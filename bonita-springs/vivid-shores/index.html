About the Community

Bonita Springs' Last Lakefront
Gated Community

Vivid Shores is a landmark in Southwest Florida real estate — a 1,031-acre master-planned lakefront community representing the last opportunity to build a gated lakefront address in Bonita Springs. The community is centered on three extraordinary freshwater lakes, formed from a former rock and sand mine and transformed into a stunning aquatic centerpiece for waterfront living and recreation.

Positioned on Bonita Grande Drive in north Bonita Springs, Vivid Shores delivers an exceptional location: two miles from Bonita Beach and Barefoot Beach Preserve, ten minutes to Downtown Bonita Springs, and convenient access to Coconut Point, Miromar Outlets, US-41, and I-75. The community sits squarely between Naples and Estero — one of the most sought-after corridors in all of Southwest Florida.

The 13-acre amenity campus is anchored by a 14,000-square-foot clubhouse, resort-style pool and spa, lakefront outdoor restaurant and bar, fitness center with saunas, and sport courts — all oriented toward the lakes. Gas-powered boats are officially permitted on 400+ acres of freshwater, making Vivid Shores one of the very few new construction communities in Bonita Springs with a true boating lifestyle.

Builders regularly offer closing cost credits, design center allowances, and interest rate buydowns to move specific inventory — but only to buyers who know to ask. These change month to month and are rarely posted anywhere.
