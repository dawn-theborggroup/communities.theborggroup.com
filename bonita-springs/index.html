About the Area

Where Coastal Living Meets
Everyday Convenience

Bonita Springs occupies one of the most enviable addresses in Southwest Florida — nestled between the Gulf and I-75, with easy access to both Naples and Fort Myers. The city is home to waterfront communities, gated golf enclaves, and family-friendly neighborhoods, all within minutes of Bonita Beach and Barefoot Beach Preserve.

The Coconut Point Mall, the Imperial River's waterfront dining district, and the community's active arts and events calendar make Bonita Springs one of the most livable cities in the region. RSW International Airport is just 20 minutes away.

Whether you're drawn by the waterfront lifestyle, championship golf, or the genuine community spirit, Bonita Springs consistently delivers. Our team knows every neighborhood here intimately.

The River District Renaissance

What's Happening in Downtown Bonita Springs?

Downtown Bonita Springs is in the middle of a genuine renaissance. After investing more than $30 million in the Old 41 corridor — new streetscaping, landscaped medians, brick-paver intersections, and infrastructure built to attract redevelopment — the city is watching that bet pay off. Live music venues, new restaurants, and riverfront projects are turning the historic district along the Imperial River into one of Southwest Florida's most talked-about downtowns.

Dining & Entertainment
A live-music district has taken root

Sugarshack Downtown anchors the corridor with live music nearly every night, and Rooftop at Riverside adds open-air dining a block away. On the waterfront, Bonita Fish Company opened in fall 2025 in the longtime Bonita Bill's location, bringing an expanded bar and a fresh menu from the team behind Doc Ford's. Downtown Coffee & Wine Co., the farmers market, the historic artist cottages, Wonder Gardens, and Arts Bonita round out a district you can genuinely spend a whole day in.

Riverside Park
The park is getting a major upgrade

Riverside Park — home of the bandshell, festivals, and Star Spangled Bonita — began a full renovation in April 2026, expected to finish by winter 2026. The redesign brings a durable artificial-turf event lawn, wider walkways for festival crowds and vendors, additional parking, bike racks, and better pedestrian connections — while preserving the live oaks, memorial trees, and public art that give the park its character. When it reopens, downtown's living room will be built for the crowds it now draws.

What's Coming Next
The pipeline keeps growing

Project Telephone is under construction at Old 41 and Hampton Street — two indoor-outdoor restaurant spaces, one built around the old telephone building that gives the project its name. HoneyHole Downtown, a live-music restaurant from the Sugarshack team, is expected in early 2027. Imperial Crossing, a Barron Collier project on the north side of the Imperial River, is planned to bring shops, restaurants, and homes, and the mixed-use Imperial 41 riverfront project envisions residences, retail, kayak launches, and river access. Even bigger picture: the proposed 11-plus-mile Bonita Estero Rail Trail could one day let residents bike from surrounding neighborhoods straight into the district.

What does this mean if you're buying in Bonita Springs? Homes and communities near Old 41 and the river district are drawing more buyer attention as downtown grows into a true destination. Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Bonita Springs compare to Naples? +
Bonita Springs offers a very similar Gulf Coast lifestyle to Naples but typically at a lower price point, especially for single-family homes. Naples tends to be more formal in atmosphere while Bonita Springs has a more relaxed, community feel. We're happy to compare both markets directly with you.
What are the top gated communities in Bonita Springs? +
Bonita Bay is one of the area's most prestigious addresses, featuring a private marina and five golf courses. Palmira and Vasari are popular golf-bundled communities known for active social scenes. Pelican Landing offers a unique private Gulf island beach club. Shadow Wood Preserve and Imperial Golf Estates are also highly regarded. We can walk you through which community aligns best with your lifestyle and budget.
Is Bonita Springs family-friendly? +
Absolutely. The Lee County School District serves Bonita Springs with several strong public options, and the area also has charter school choices. The community has excellent parks, youth sports leagues, and family-oriented amenities throughout.
What flood risk exists in Bonita Springs? +
Flood risk varies significantly by neighborhood. Areas along the Imperial River carry higher risk while inland communities on higher ground have lower exposure. Always check the FEMA Flood Map for any specific property. We discuss flood zones, elevation certificates, and insurance implications with every buyer.
Are there new construction options in Bonita Springs? +
Yes — Bonita Springs has active new construction including Vivid Shores and Bonita Lakes. The neighboring Estero corridor also has significant new inventory at Verdana Village, WildBlue, and Kingston. We strongly recommend speaking with us before visiting any sales office, as builder representatives work for the builder exclusively.
How far is Bonita Springs from the beach? +
Most Bonita Springs neighborhoods are within 10–20 minutes of Bonita Beach Park and Barefoot Beach Preserve — some of the finest Gulf access in all of Southwest Florida.
What is happening in downtown Bonita Springs? +
Downtown Bonita Springs is undergoing a major revitalization. The city has invested over $30 million in the Old 41 corridor, and it shows — Sugarshack Downtown and Rooftop at Riverside anchor a growing live-music and dining scene, Riverside Park is being renovated through winter 2026 with a new event lawn and wider walkways, and projects like Project Telephone, HoneyHole Downtown, and Imperial Crossing are bringing new restaurants, retail, and riverfront living. It's one of the most exciting growth stories in Southwest Florida right now, and we're happy to show you the neighborhoods closest to it.
