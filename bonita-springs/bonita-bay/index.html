The Quick Answer

What is Bonita Bay?

Bonita Bay is a 2,400-acre gated, master-planned community on the west side of U.S. 41 in Bonita Springs, stretching along the Imperial River toward Estero Bay. It has defined luxury living in the Bonita Springs–Estero corridor for more than thirty years, and it remains the most complete community in the area: roughly 3,300 residences across more than 50 distinct neighborhoods, with more than half of the land preserved as nature areas, lakes, and open space.

What surprises most buyers is the range. Bonita Bay's reputation says multi-million-dollar estates — and those are here — but the same gates, security, pathways, parks, and beach access come with coach homes and condominiums at far more attainable prices. It's one community with dozens of ways to live in it.

Amenities

What do Bonita Bay residents actually get?

Start with what every resident enjoys through the community itself: 24-hour manned gates, three waterfront parks, kayak launches, and a 12-mile network of walking and biking pathways threading through preserves and lakes — you can genuinely live here and do your daily exercise without seeing a car. The private beach park on Little Hickory Island puts Gulf sand a short drive or seasonal shuttle away, with pavilions, chair and umbrella service, and facilities rebuilt after Hurricane Ian to modern elevation standards.

Then there's the Bonita Bay Club — the private club offering five championship golf courses (three inside the community, two at its east location) designed by Arthur Hills and Tom Fazio, plus tennis, pickleball, a spa and fitness lifestyle center, and multiple dining venues. A major, nine-figure club renovation is underway right now, continuing a long pattern of reinvestment that protects both the experience and property values. And for boaters, the full-service marina — with roughly 90 wet slips, dry storage for about 330 boats up to 36 feet, and Backwater Jack's waterfront restaurant — offers direct access to Estero Bay and the Gulf.

Location

Where is Bonita Bay, and what's nearby?

Bonita Bay sits between Naples and Fort Myers on U.S. 41, which means residents borrow the best of both without belonging entirely to either — Naples' dining and culture roughly half an hour south, RSW airport about half an hour north. You don't even need to leave the neighborhood's orbit for a good evening: the Promenade at Bonita Bay sits just outside the gates with restaurants, boutiques, and salons, and the revitalized downtown Bonita Springs river district — with its live music venues and growing restaurant scene — is minutes away.

Homes & Neighborhoods

What kinds of homes are in Bonita Bay?

More than 50 neighborhoods give Bonita Bay a wider range than any comparable community in Southwest Florida. Coach homes and garden condominiums — often with golf, preserve, or lake views — can start in the $250,000s to $300,000s, making them one of the region's most affordable doors into a truly luxury master plan. Mid-rise and high-rise towers deliver panoramic views over Estero Bay and the Gulf. And custom single-family homes, from golf-course villas to waterfront estates near the marina, carry the community's top end well into the millions.

Each neighborhood has its own sub-association, character, and price logic — a high-rise with sunset views is a completely different purchase than an estate on the Fazio course. Tell us how you want to live and we'll narrow 50-plus neighborhoods down to the two or three that genuinely fit.

The Fine Print

What should buyers know about fees and membership?

Here's the detail that trips up buyers who don't have local guidance: owning in Bonita Bay and belonging to the Bonita Bay Club are two different things. Every owner joins the Bonita Bay Community Association — the master association that runs security, roads, parks, pathways, and preserves — plus their neighborhood's sub-association. Club membership for golf, tennis, spa, and dining is separate and optional, with its own levels, costs, and availability that change over time.

That structure is a feature, not a flaw — it's why Bonita Bay can house both the avid golfer and the boater who never picks up a club. But it means the true cost of any specific home is a three-layer question: master association, sub-association, and whichever club membership fits your life. We put exact, current numbers on all three for every home our buyers consider — before the offer, never after.

Common Questions

Bonita Bay, Answered

Is club membership included when you buy in Bonita Bay?
No — and buyers are often surprised by this. Every homeowner belongs to the Bonita Bay Community Association, which covers security, roads, parks, pathways, and the community's natural areas. Golf, tennis, spa, and dining run separately through the private Bonita Bay Club, with its own membership levels and waitlists that change over time. That separation is why we verify current club availability and costs for every buyer before an offer is written.
Does Bonita Bay have private beach access?
Yes. Residents enjoy a private beach park on Little Hickory Island along Bonita Beach, with pavilions, chairs and umbrellas, restrooms, and a seasonal shuttle from the community. The park was fully rebuilt after Hurricane Ian — elevated 14 feet above sea level to modern code — and private Gulf access like this is one of the rarest amenities in Southwest Florida.
Can I keep a boat in Bonita Bay?
Yes — Bonita Bay's full-service marina offers roughly 90 wet slips and dry storage for about 330 boats up to 36 feet, with direct access to Estero Bay and the Gulf. Backwater Jack's, the casual waterfront restaurant at the marina, is a favorite gathering spot residents can reach by boat, bike, or the community's pathways.
What types of homes are in Bonita Bay, and what do they cost?
More than 50 distinct neighborhoods hold roughly 3,300 residences — condominiums and coach homes that can start in the $250,000s–$300,000s, mid-rise and high-rise towers with panoramic bay and Gulf views, and custom single-family estates on golf or water that reach well into the millions. That range means Bonita Bay is more attainable than its reputation suggests, while still anchoring the luxury market.
How many golf courses does Bonita Bay have?
Five championship courses through the Bonita Bay Club — three inside the community and two at the club's east location — designed by Arthur Hills and Tom Fazio. A major club renovation is currently underway, continuing the club's long record of reinvestment.
Is Bonita Bay gated and secure?
Yes — Bonita Bay is a gated community with 24-hour manned security, and the master association maintains the roads, landscaping, preserves, and the 12-mile pathway network throughout the 2,400 acres.
