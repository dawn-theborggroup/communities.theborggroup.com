About the Area

Florida's Premier Luxury Destination

Naples stands apart as one of the finest places to live in all of America. With pristine Gulf beaches, world-class dining on 5th Avenue South, and an arts scene that rivals cities three times its size, Naples sets the standard for luxury living in Southwest Florida — and consistently ranks among the best places to retire and relocate in the country.

The real estate market spans from elegant Gulf-front condominiums in Pelican Bay to ultra-exclusive waterfront estates in Port Royal, where properties regularly trade at $10 million and well beyond. Golf communities, country clubs, and preserve-front neighborhoods round out a market that truly offers something for every luxury buyer.

For relocating buyers, Naples offers exceptional healthcare, top-tier schools, a vibrant social calendar, and a pace of life that is simultaneously refined and genuinely relaxed. Our team works across all Naples neighborhoods and can match you with the right community.

Why Buyers Choose Naples

Naples residents enjoy an unmatched quality of life — sunset strolls on the pier, gallery openings on 3rd Street, mornings on championship fairways, and evenings at restaurants that rival any major metropolitan area.

Medical Care

Healthcare in Naples

Naples is home to two major healthcare systems with a combined network of hospitals, specialty practices, and outpatient centers that serve the entire Collier County region.

NCH Healthcare System

Naples · Two Hospitals

NCH Healthcare System operates NCH Baker Hospital Downtown and NCH North Naples Hospital, along with a comprehensive network of specialty practices and urgent care facilities. NCH has cultivated a national reputation for cardiac care and neurosciences.

Two hospitals · Cardiac & neuro specialty · Cancer care

Physicians Regional Medical Center

Naples · Two Locations

Physicians Regional operates two full-service hospital campuses in Naples — Pine Ridge and Collier Boulevard — providing a comprehensive range of surgical specialties, emergency care, orthopedics, and women's health services.

Two campuses · Surgical specialties · Emergency & ortho

Specialty Care & Private Medicine

Concierge & Specialist Network

Naples has an exceptionally well-developed private practice and concierge medicine ecosystem. Virtually every major specialty is represented locally, and concierge medicine practices are common among year-round and seasonal residents.

Concierge medicine · Full specialist network · Urgent care

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Naples only for ultra-high-net-worth buyers? +
Not at all. While Naples has one of the highest concentrations of luxury real estate in Florida, there is a broad spectrum of price points — from condos in the high $400s to $20M+ waterfront estates. Many buyers are surprised by what their budget can access here. Browse current listings to see the full range.
What are the best neighborhoods in Naples? +
Port Royal and Aqualane Shores lead the ultra-luxury waterfront segment. Pelican Bay is beloved for its private Gulf beach clubs and diverse offerings. Park Shore, the Moorings, and Olde Naples offer wonderful waterfront and walkable options. For golf, Grey Oaks, Mediterra, Quail West, and Talis Park are among the most sought-after. The right neighborhood depends entirely on your lifestyle priorities.
How are Naples schools? +
Collier County Public Schools includes several A-rated schools and ranks among Florida's better-performing large districts. Naples also has outstanding private schools including Community School of Naples, First Baptist Academy, and Seacrest Country Day School, plus high-performing charter options like Mason Classical Academy.
How do golf community memberships work in Naples? +
Naples has 70+ golf courses and dozens of private club communities. Membership structures range from equity-based (where you own a share of the club) to non-equity and bundled memberships that come with home purchase. Initiation fees, monthly dues, and waitlists vary significantly by community. This is one of the most important factors in selecting a golf community — we can walk you through every option.
Does Naples have a second airport? +
Yes. In addition to RSW International Airport roughly 35 minutes away, Naples has its own Naples Airport (APF) right in the city — primarily used for private aviation and charter flights. This makes Naples particularly convenient for buyers who travel by private aircraft.
Is Naples a good investment market? +
Historically, yes. Naples has one of the strongest long-term appreciation records in Florida, driven by genuinely limited Gulf-front land supply, sustained high-income buyer demand, and a luxury lifestyle that consistently attracts relocating buyers from across the country.
