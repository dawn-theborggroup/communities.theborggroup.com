The Quick Answer

What is Barefoot Beach?

Barefoot Beach is a roughly 460-acre gated community occupying the southern tip of Little Hickory Island, bounded by the Gulf of Mexico on one side and Little Hickory Bay on the other, with the 342-acre Barefoot Beach Preserve as its southern neighbor. Technically it sits in unincorporated Collier County — Collier schools, Collier services — yet it carries a Bonita Springs mailing address and is reached only through Bonita Springs via Bonita Beach Road, which is why it belongs to the Bonita Beach story no matter what the county line says.

Six distinct enclaves live behind the gate, from mid-rise Gulf-view condominiums to double-gated bayfront estates with boat lifts — and every single residence, condo or estate, bayside or beachside, comes with deeded access to the same celebrated white-sand beach. It is Southwest Florida barrier-island living in its most complete form.

The Six Enclaves

Which Barefoot Beach enclave fits your life?

Condominiums

Barefoot Beach Club

348 residences in 12 mid-rise buildings — eight Gulf-front — built 1991–94, with two pools, a beachfront clubhouse and fitness center, and boardwalks to the sand. The widest price range and deepest inventory in the community.

Villas

The Villas at Barefoot Beach

Fifty tri-level villas steps from the beach, nine directly Gulf-front — 1,700 to 2,800 square feet with garages, two pools, and lock-and-go association living.

Gulf-Front Estates

Beach Gardens

Estate homes arranged in individually named gardens in a horseshoe around a shared pool — every home with Gulf views, the front row directly on the sand, plus a private clubhouse with tennis and boat slips.

The Rarest

The Cottages

Fifteen Gulf-front single-family homes at the community's south end beside the Preserve. Inventory almost never turns — when one lists, decisions happen fast.

Bayfront Boating

Bayfront Gardens

Twenty-three single-family homes on Hickory Bay with private docks, preserved land across the street, and no-bridge Gulf access from the backyard.

Double-Gated

Southport on the Bay

106 homes behind their own second gate — most with docks and lifts, plus a private community marina — winding along Southport Cove, San Mateo, Malibu Cove, and Topanga.

The Club

How does The Club at Barefoot Beach work — and how do you skip the waitlist?

At the community's south end sits The Club at Barefoot Beach: a private, member-owned beach club with full beach and pool service, casual and fine dining, Har-Tru tennis, an active social calendar, and a marina with slips available for annual lease. Membership is optional — but it has become one of the most sought-after club memberships on the coast, and the waitlist can run years. A renovation and expansion of the club has been approved, which tends to make demand stronger, not weaker.

Here's the insight that separates local guidance from internet browsing: waitlists can sometimes be bypassed when a buyer purchases from a seller who holds a transferable membership and resigns it at closing. That makes a listing's membership status a real part of its value — two similar condos can be very different purchases if one carries a transferable membership. We verify membership status on every Barefoot Beach property our buyers consider, and for sellers, a transferable membership is a marketing asset we make sure the market knows about.

Location & The Preserve

Where is Barefoot Beach, and what's it next to?

The only way in is through Bonita Springs: west on Bonita Beach Road, then south past the guardhouse onto the island. That approach is the point — Barefoot Beach sits apart from everything while being minutes from it all. Doc's Beach House and the restaurants of Bonita Beach are just up the road, downtown Bonita Springs' dining and music scene is a short drive, Mercato and North Naples shopping sit conveniently south, and RSW airport is about forty minutes.

And then there's the southern neighbor: Barefoot Beach Preserve, 342 acres of protected shoreline and gopher-tortoise habitat regularly ranked among America's best beaches. Living beside a preserve means the sand south of you will never grow condos — a permanence that quietly protects both the experience and the property values inside the gate.

Homes & The Fine Print

What do homes cost, and what should buyers verify?

The enclaves span the whole luxury spectrum: condominiums and villas from around $1 million into the low millions, Southport on the Bay homes from roughly $2 million to the mid-$5 millions, and the Gulf-front estates of Beach Gardens and The Cottages from near $4.5 million to beyond $16 million. Inventory is thin, seasonal, and moves on relationships as much as listings — several of the best Barefoot Beach sales each year happen quietly.

What we verify before any offer: the enclave's association fees and what they cover (condo fees here often include insurance), rental policies where income matters, dock rights and lift specifics on bayside homes, elevation and insurance realities on a barrier island, and — always — club membership status and transferability. Barefoot Beach rewards buyers who know exactly what they're buying; that's what we're for.

Common Questions

Barefoot Beach, Answered

Is Barefoot Beach in Bonita Springs or Naples?
Both answers are half right — which is why the internet argues about it. Barefoot Beach sits at the southern tip of Little Hickory Island in unincorporated Collier County, so it uses Collier services and schools and many owners think of it as North Naples. But it carries a Bonita Springs mailing address and is reached only through Bonita Springs via Bonita Beach Road. Locally, we treat it as the crown of the Bonita Beach corridor.
What are the six enclaves of Barefoot Beach?
Behind the main gate: Barefoot Beach Club (348 condos in 12 mid-rise buildings), The Villas at Barefoot Beach (50 tri-level villas steps from the sand), Beach Gardens (Gulf-front estate homes arranged in individually named gardens, each with its own pool), The Cottages (15 Gulf-front homes that almost never trade), Bayfront Gardens (23 single-family homes on Hickory Bay with private docks), and Southport on the Bay (106 homes behind their own second gate, most with docks and lifts). Each enclave is a different lifestyle and price point.
How does membership in The Club at Barefoot Beach work?
The Club at Barefoot Beach is a private, member-owned beach club at the community's south end — full beach and pool service, casual and fine dining, Har-Tru tennis, and a marina with slips available for annual lease. Membership is optional and currently in high demand, with a waitlist that can run years. Here's the local insight: buyers can often bypass the wait by purchasing from a seller who holds a transferable membership and resigns it at closing — which makes membership status a genuine factor in which home you choose. We verify it on every Barefoot Beach listing our buyers consider.
Does every home in Barefoot Beach have beach access?
Yes — deeded beach access comes with every residence in Barefoot Beach, whether it's a bayside home in Southport or a Gulf-front condo. Boardwalks cross the dunes at intervals along the community, and the beach itself is the same soft white sand that made neighboring Barefoot Beach Preserve one of the most celebrated stretches of shoreline in Florida.
Can you keep a boat in Barefoot Beach?
Barefoot Beach is genuinely a boater's community. Bayside homes in Southport on the Bay and Bayfront Gardens typically have private docks and lifts on Little Hickory Bay, with no-bridge access to the Gulf. Southport also has a private community marina, The Club offers slips for annual lease, and additional storage is available at the boat club across the bay. If boating is central to your search, tell us — dock specifics vary meaningfully home to home.
What do homes in Barefoot Beach cost?
The range is wide because the enclaves are so different: condominiums and villas generally run from around $1 million into the low millions, Southport on the Bay homes trade from roughly $2 million to the mid-$5 millions, and the Gulf-front estates of Beach Gardens and The Cottages start near $4.5 million and have exceeded $16 million. Inventory is thin and seasonal, so timing matters as much as budget here.
