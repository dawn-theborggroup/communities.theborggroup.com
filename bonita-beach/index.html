About the Area

Waterfront Living on the Gulf

Bonita Beach is one of the most sought-after waterfront destinations in Southwest Florida — positioned along the Gulf between Naples and Fort Myers Beach with pristine white-sand shores, boating access through Estero Bay, and a relaxed coastal lifestyle that draws buyers from across the country.

Whether you're searching for a Gulf-front condominium, a custom waterfront estate with a private dock, or a smart investment property, Bonita Beach consistently delivers. Natural beauty, walkability, and proximity to top dining and entertainment make it one of the region's most enduring markets.

For those relocating to Southwest Florida, Bonita Beach represents exactly the lifestyle most buyers imagine when they dream of living in Florida. Reach out to our team — we know every building and street here intimately.

Why Buyers Choose Bonita Beach

Many homeowners describe mornings walking the shoreline, afternoons boating through Estero Bay, and evenings at waterfront restaurants watching the sun sink into the Gulf.

Know the Bonita Beach Market

Bonita Beach is a small, high-demand coastal market. Understanding the distinct neighborhoods and buildings — and what makes each unique — is essential to finding the right fit.

Gulf-Front Condos — Bonita Beach Rd
Little Hickory Island · Beachfront

Buildings such as Bonita Beach Club, Barefoot Pelican, and Bonita Surf offer direct Gulf frontage, private beach access, and resort-style amenities. Supply is permanently constrained by the barrier island setting, which has historically supported price resilience.

Bonita Shores
Established · No HOA · Boat Access

One of the area's most beloved established neighborhoods — a no-HOA community of single-family homes with canal access to Estero Bay and the Gulf. Residents enjoy the freedom of no association restrictions alongside a genuine neighborhood feel.

Bayfront · Deep Water · Custom Homes

Homes here sit directly on the bay with sweeping water views, deep-water dockage, and near-instant access to the Gulf via Estero Bay. Many are custom-built estates with outdoor living spaces designed around the water.

Medical Care

Healthcare Near Bonita Beach

Bonita Beach residents have access to two major health systems within a short drive, along with a growing network of specialty and urgent care facilities along the US-41 corridor.

NCH Healthcare System

Naples · ~20 min South

NCH operates two full-service hospitals in Naples with a comprehensive network of outpatient facilities, specialist offices, and urgent care centers. NCH Baker Hospital is the regional leader in cardiac care and neuroscience.

Two hospitals · Full specialist network · Cancer care

Lee Health — Gulf Coast Medical Center

Fort Myers · ~25 min North

Gulf Coast Medical Center is Lee Health's flagship hospital and one of Florida's highest-rated facilities. It features a Level II Trauma Center, award-winning cardiac surgery, a comprehensive cancer center, and a dedicated women's health pavilion.

Level II Trauma · Cardiac surgery · Cancer center

Physicians Regional & Urgent Care

US-41 Corridor · Convenient Access

Physicians Regional operates two hospitals in the Naples area and a growing network of walk-in care and specialty practices. The US-41 corridor between Bonita Beach and Naples has multiple urgent care centers for routine and non-emergency needs.

Two hospitals · Walk-in care on US-41 · Specialty practices

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bonita Beach a good place to live full-time? +
Absolutely. Many residents live here year-round and enjoy walkable beach access, boating, and a genuine sense of community. The area's proximity to shopping, healthcare, and RSW International Airport makes it a very practical choice for full-time coastal living, not just seasonal.
What are home prices like on Bonita Beach? +
Beachfront condominiums typically range from the high $400s to well over $2 million depending on floor, views, and building. Luxury waterfront homes range from $1.5M to $5M or more. Canal-front homes with boating access generally run $800K–$2.5M. Browse current listings to see what's available now.
Are there HOA fees for condominiums? +
Yes — most condo buildings carry monthly HOA fees ranging from a few hundred to over $2,000 per month depending on amenities, building age, and reserves. Contact us for a full breakdown on any specific building you're considering.
What flood zone is Bonita Beach in? +
Most of Bonita Beach falls within FEMA-designated flood zones (typically Zone AE or VE for Gulf-front properties), which means flood insurance is required for most mortgaged purchases. We help every buyer understand and budget for this before making an offer — it's one of the most important considerations in this market.
Is Bonita Beach good for short-term rental investment? +
It can be an excellent investment. Bonita Beach sees strong seasonal demand from October through April. However, building-specific rental restriction policies vary significantly — some buildings allow weekly rentals, others require 30-day minimums. Our team can identify which buildings allow short-term rentals and help you evaluate realistic income potential before you commit.
How close is Bonita Beach to RSW Airport? +
Southwest Florida International Airport (RSW) is approximately 20–25 minutes from Bonita Beach — making it one of the most convenient beachfront locations in the region for frequent travelers and part-time residents flying in seasonally.
