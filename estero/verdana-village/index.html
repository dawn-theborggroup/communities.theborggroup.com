About the Community

Resort Amenities. New Construction Value.
Prime Estero Location.

Verdana Village is one of Southwest Florida's most ambitious new construction communities — a master-planned development centered around a resort amenity package that is genuinely rare in residential real estate. The centerpiece is a climate-controlled indoor sports complex featuring pickleball, tennis, and basketball courts, complemented by a resort lagoon pool, spa, fitness center, and The Shoppes at Verdana, an on-site retail and dining district.

The West Village Amenity Center — featuring a second resort pool, lap pool, spa, fitness center, clubhouse, and sports fields — is now open. An East Village Amenity Center is planned, giving residents three distinct amenity complexes across the community.

Located along Corkscrew Road in Estero, Verdana Village places residents minutes from Miromar Outlets, Hertz Arena, Florida Gulf Coast University, and a short drive to Gulf beaches. RSW International Airport is approximately 20 minutes away.

Key Access Points

Corkscrew Road at I-75 · ~20 min to RSW · ~10 min to Miromar Outlets · ~25 min to Bonita Beach · ~35 min to Naples 5th Avenue

Infrastructure & Growth

The Alico Road Extension:
A Game-Changer for Estero

Lee County approved a $26.8 million contract in May 2025 to extend Alico Road westward approximately 2.5 miles, connecting through the Corkscrew Road corridor — the exact area where Verdana Village sits. Construction is expected to begin mid-2026 with completion targeted for late 2027 to 2028. This extension will significantly reduce east-west travel times and directly improve access to and from Verdana Village.

Buyers purchasing now may benefit from appreciation driven by this infrastructure improvement.

Reduced Commute Times

The extension creates a new east-west route reducing dependence on congested US-41 and Ben Hill Griffin Pkwy, cutting drive times to Fort Myers, RSW, and I-75 south for Verdana residents.

Catalyst for Corridor Growth

Major road infrastructure attracts additional commercial and residential development. The Corkscrew–Alico corridor is already one of Lee County's fastest-growing areas; this extension accelerates that trajectory.

Long-Term Value Driver

Proximity to planned infrastructure improvements is a well-documented factor in residential appreciation. Buyers at Verdana Village today are positioned ahead of the improved connectivity that will make this location even more desirable.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does using a buyer's agent at Verdana Village cost me anything? +
No. Buyer's agent representation is completely free to you at Verdana Village. The builder pays The Borg Group's commission — it is not added to your purchase price. You receive full professional representation, contract review, negotiation support, and ongoing advocacy at zero cost.
What are the CDD fees at Verdana Village? +
Verdana Village has a Community Development District (CDD). CDD fees are billed as part of your annual property tax notice — they are not a separate monthly HOA charge. The amount varies by home type and lot. Contact us for current estimated CDD fees by product type.
What floor plans are currently available? +
The builders offer multiple floor plans ranging from approximately 1,400 to over 3,400 square feet across villa and single-family collections. Available floor plans, lot inventory, and quick move-in options change frequently as phases are released. Reach out to us for a current list with pricing.
What is the flood risk at Verdana Village? +
Verdana Village is located inland along Corkscrew Road, away from tidal flooding risk. Most homes in the community fall in favorable flood zones. We recommend reviewing the current FEMA flood map and obtaining a flood elevation certificate for your specific lot — we can assist with this process.
What is the Alico Road extension and how does it affect Verdana Village? +
Lee County approved a $26.8 million contract in May 2025 to extend Alico Road westward through the Corkscrew corridor, with construction expected to begin mid-2026 and targeted completion in 2027–2028. This new east-west road will reduce travel times for Verdana Village residents to Fort Myers, RSW Airport, and I-75 south.
Is Verdana Village a good investment property? +
Verdana Village has attracted significant investor interest, and rental demand in the Estero corridor is strong. However, HOA and CDD rules on short-term rentals apply — always review the governing documents before purchasing as an investment. Contact us for a detailed analysis including rental restrictions and estimated rental income ranges.
Can I sell my Verdana Village home while the builder is still selling new construction? +
Yes — and it's more common than you might expect. Move-in-ready availability, upgraded landscaping, window treatments, and owner improvements are genuine advantages that motivate buyers who don't want to wait for a build timeline. Pricing strategy is critical — we analyze current builder base prices, available incentives, and resale comps before recommending a list price.
What upgrades add the most value when selling in Verdana Village? +
In a community where buyers can compare your home against builder-standard finishes, upgrades that are visible and functional carry the most weight — extended lanais, private pools, premium flooring, designer kitchens, and whole-home generators. We walk every seller through which improvements to highlight and how to present them.
How do I know what my Verdana Village home is worth with builders still selling nearby? +
The valuation process requires looking at both resale comps and builder pricing simultaneously. We factor in your home's phase, lot position, upgrades, and the current builder incentive environment to arrive at a price that's competitive without leaving money on the table. Contact us for a no-obligation valuation.
