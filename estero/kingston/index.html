About the Community

Southwest Florida's Most Ambitious
Master-Planned Community

Kingston is in a class of its own. Spanning more than 7,600 acres along eastern Corkscrew Road in Estero — east of Verdana Village and stretching toward SR-82 — Kingston is the largest and most transformative master-planned community ever introduced to Southwest Florida.

Developed by Cameratta Companies, the Estero-based family firm behind The Place at Corkscrew and Corkscrew Shores, Kingston represents decades of local expertise applied to a generational vision. The community will deliver approximately 10,000 residences and nearly 700,000 square feet of commercial space.

Ground broke November 13, 2025, and Kingston celebrated its grand opening on October 17, 2026, with decorated models from all four Phase 1 builders: Lennar, Pulte, Neal Communities, and Taylor Morrison. Builders already have move-in-ready and under-construction homes available.

Reason 1

You Must Name Your Agent Before You Visit

In Florida, the moment you walk into a builder's sales office without naming a buyer's agent, you may permanently forfeit your right to independent representation at that community. It doesn't matter if you haven't signed anything yet.

Reason 2

First-Phase Strategy Requires Local Knowledge

In large master-planned communities, the first phases often offer the best pricing — but not always the best lots. Understanding which homesites position well for long-term value takes local knowledge that doesn't come from the builder's sales sheet.

Reason 3

Builder Incentives Are Negotiation Tools

Closing cost credits, design center allowances, and rate buydowns are not gifts — they're sales tools the builder deploys strategically. Knowing when an incentive is available requires experience working deals across multiple builders in this market.

Reason 4

Builder Contracts Protect the Builder

Every builder contract is written by the builder's legal team to protect the builder's interests. Your contingency rights, cancellation terms, and what happens if the builder delays are all in that document — and won't be explained to you by the builder's agent.

Reason 5

Independent Financing Means Real Comparison

Builders push buyers toward preferred lenders because it streamlines the transaction for them. That lender may not offer the best rate or terms available to you. We help you compare financing options independently.

Reason 6

Build-Out Takes Time — You Need Someone Who Stays

Kingston won't be built overnight. Walk-throughs, punch lists, framing inspections, builder delays, and closing day logistics all come after the paperwork. You need a representative who stays engaged from contract to keys.

Infrastructure & Growth

The Eastern Corkscrew Corridor
Is Being Built for Kingston's Future

Kingston is not just a residential development — it is the catalyst for a fundamental transformation of the eastern Estero corridor. Lee County, the Village of Estero, and FDOT are all actively investing in roads, intersections, and infrastructure that will serve this community for decades.

Multiple publicly funded projects are under construction or approved that directly serve the Kingston area.

Corkscrew Road Widening — Phase 2

A ~$27 million road widening project actively under construction in the eastern Estero corridor, with completion expected by late 2026. Adds a 10-foot-wide shared-use path on the south side and a 6-foot sidewalk on the north.

Kingston Village Parkway to SR-82

A new Kingston Village Parkway will connect Corkscrew Road directly to SR-82. The new intersection signal is targeted for Fall 2026, with full SR-82 connectivity expected Winter 2027/2028.

Alico Road Extension

Lee County approved a $26.8 million contract in May 2025 to extend Alico Road westward through the Corkscrew corridor, construction beginning mid-2026. This new east-west connector reduces travel times to Fort Myers, RSW Airport, and I-75 south.

Does Kingston have a CDD? +
Yes. Kingston is in a Community Development District (Kingston One CDD), so homeowners pay an annual CDD assessment on their property tax bill in addition to HOA dues. The amount depends on the neighborhood and homesite size. Ask us for the current figures for the homes you're considering before you sign.
How large is Kingston and how long will it take to build out? +
Kingston spans more than 7,600 acres, with about 10,000 planned residences and 700,000 square feet of commercial space along eastern Corkscrew Road in Estero, FL. More than 3,750 acres are set aside as preserves. Full build-out will unfold over many years and multiple phases.
What infrastructure improvements are planned near Kingston? +
Three major public infrastructure projects are underway: (1) A $27 million Corkscrew Road widening in active construction, targeted for completion late 2026; (2) The new Kingston Village Parkway to SR-82, with signal targeted for Fall 2026 and full connectivity by Winter 2027/2028; and (3) The Alico Road extension ($26.8M, approved May 2025) improving east-west access to Fort Myers and RSW Airport.
Will there be schools within Kingston? +
The developer has identified a parcel within Kingston for a future K–8 school. Additionally, True North Classical Academy — a tuition-free K–12 charter school planned for 3,100 students — is anticipated to open in the 2028/2029 school year in the Corkscrew corridor. Lee County public schools currently serve the area.
