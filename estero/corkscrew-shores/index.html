About the Community

Live Like You're on Vacation —
Every Single Day

Corkscrew Shores is a 722-acre gated master-planned community in Estero, developed by Cameratta Companies and built by Pulte Homes. The community's centerpiece is a spectacular 240-acre lake — stocked with sport fish and open to non-motorized and electric watercraft — that creates the kind of waterfront lifestyle that most buyers can only find at a much higher price point.

Two things make Corkscrew Shores stand out immediately: no CDD fee and not in a flood zone. In a market where CDDs routinely add $2,000–$5,000+ per year to ownership costs and flood insurance can be a significant expense, these advantages translate directly to lower total cost of ownership and meaningfully better value.

Located on Corkscrew Road three miles east of I-75, the community is positioned within minutes of Miromar Outlets, Gulf Coast Town Center, RSW International Airport, and Florida Gulf Coast University. The Corkscrew Road corridor is one of the most active growth zones in Southwest Florida — and Corkscrew Shores sits at the heart of it.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Corkscrew Shores have a CDD fee? +
No. Corkscrew Shores has no CDD (Community Development District) fee — a meaningful advantage that reduces total ownership cost compared to many nearby communities that carry CDD assessments on top of HOA fees. In communities with CDDs, buyers often pay an additional $1,500–$4,000+ per year. At Corkscrew Shores, your amenity cost is simply the standard HOA.
Is Corkscrew Shores in a flood zone? +
No. Corkscrew Shores is in Flood Zone X — generally not in a FEMA-designated flood zone. This means flood insurance is generally not required by lenders, which is a meaningful cost advantage. Always verify for any specific parcel before making an offer, as flood zone designations can differ within a community. We review this as a standard part of our buyer process.
Can you boat on the Corkscrew Shores lake? +
Yes — non-motorized and electric-motor watercraft only. Gas-powered boats are not permitted, which keeps the lake clean and peaceful. Residents can kayak, canoe, paddleboard, and use small electric boats. The lake is also stocked with sport fish and has a fishing dock. It's a highly active and beautiful amenity that defines daily life in the community.
Is Corkscrew Shores still building new construction? +
Corkscrew Shores is nearing buildout. Pulte may have a limited number of remaining homesites or quick move-in homes available. Contact us before visiting any sales office — we track what's actually available and whether it represents fair value in the current market. If new construction is no longer available, the resale market often has upgraded homes at competitive prices.
How does Corkscrew Shores compare to WildBlue and Verdana Village? +
All three are on the Corkscrew Road corridor and center around water amenities. WildBlue is newer with a larger lake and stronger lakefront home availability; Verdana Village's differentiator is the indoor sports complex. Corkscrew Shores' advantages are No CDD, No flood zone, and the Captain's Club waterfront restaurant — things that affect the total cost of ownership and daily lifestyle meaningfully. We can walk you through a direct side-by-side comparison.
What Pulte floor plans are most popular at Corkscrew Shores? +
The most frequently seen floor plans are the Martin Ray (2,107 SF, 3 BR + den), Mystique (1,889 SF, 3 BR), Pinnacle (2,488 SF, 3 BR + den), and Stonewater (3,925 SF, 3–4 BR with loft). The Clubview (3,078 SF, 4 BR + den) is the community's largest single-story option. Many resale buyers favor the Pinnacle and Stonewater for the extra flex space.
