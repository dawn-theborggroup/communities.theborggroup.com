About the Community

Where Every Day Feels Like
a Resort Vacation

The Place at Corkscrew delivers the most resort-lifestyle experience per dollar in the Estero market — a 100-foot waterslide, a full-service restaurant and bourbon bar, a two-story fitness center, a spa, and courts for every sport, all without golf fees. On more than 1,000 acres off Corkscrew Road, roughly 1,325 single-family homes by Lennar and Pulte are organized around an amenity campus that is genuinely unusual for a residential community at this price point.

The Barefoot Bar & Grill means residents walk to dinner without getting in a car. The bourbon bar provides a gathering place for adults. The two-story fitness center, movement studio, and day spa mean that health and wellness is woven into the daily routine. For families, the splash pad, playground, and childwatch services make the amenity campus genuinely usable — not just impressive on a brochure.

Most properties sit in Flood Zone X, meaning flood insurance is not required for most buyers — a meaningful financial advantage in coastal Southwest Florida. The community is approximately 20 minutes from RSW International Airport and close to Miromar Outlets, Gulf Coast Town Center, and Coconut Point Mall.

Note on Golf Nearby

The former Old Corkscrew Golf Club site across the street is being redeveloped into The Pandion Club — a private members-only club opening late 2027. Public golf access at that site will not continue.

What Makes This Community Unique

If you've been searching The Place at Corkscrew, the clubhouse, or the Barefoot Bar and Grill — you already know this community stands apart. Here's the full picture, plus what it actually means to buy here.

Barefoot Bar & Grill

A full restaurant and bar — inside the gate.

The Barefoot Bar and Grill is one of the most distinctive amenities in any Estero gated community — a full-service indoor/outdoor restaurant and bourbon bar residents can walk or golf-cart to. Friday night dinners, weekend brunches, and happy hours without leaving the neighborhood. Very few communities anywhere in Southwest Florida offer this.

Pandion Golf Club

Golf access without mandatory membership.

Pandion Golf Club is the public golf course adjacent to The Place at Corkscrew, giving residents convenient golf access without the mandatory membership fees that burden many Southwest Florida golf communities. For buyers who want golf available — but not required — this setup is exactly right.

The Clubhouse

Resort amenities that rival five-star properties.

The Place clubhouse anchors a resort amenity package that includes a 100-foot waterslide, resort pool with zero-entry beach area, spa, fitness center, café, tennis and pickleball courts, and a movement studio. For families and active lifestyle buyers, there are very few communities in Estero that match this package at this price point.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Place at Corkscrew in Estero? +
The Place at Corkscrew is a gated master-planned community in Estero, Florida with approximately 1,325 single-family homes built by Lennar and Pulte. It is known for the most extensive resort-style amenity campus in the Corkscrew Road corridor — including a 100-foot waterslide, full-service restaurant and bourbon bar, two-story fitness center, and spa. No golf course. No mandatory golf fees.
Does The Place at Corkscrew have a waterslide? +
Yes — a 100-foot resort waterslide is the signature amenity of the pool complex. The pool area also includes a beach-entry resort pool with rock waterfalls, a 40-person spa, a splash pad for children, cabanas, and poolside food and beverage service.
Does The Place at Corkscrew have a restaurant? +
Yes. The Barefoot Bar & Grill is a full-service restaurant with indoor and outdoor seating available to residents. The amenity campus also includes a bourbon bar, a marketplace café, and a poolside outdoor bar. On-site dining of this quality is unusual in any residential community at this price point.
Does The Place at Corkscrew have golf? +
The Place at Corkscrew does not have an internal golf course and there are no golf fees or mandatory memberships. The former Old Corkscrew Golf Club site directly across the street is currently being redeveloped into The Pandion Club — a new private, golf-only club with a capped membership of 280, designed by Kyle Phillips and scheduled to open in late 2027. The public golf access that previously existed at that site will not continue once The Pandion Club opens.
Is The Place at Corkscrew in a flood zone? +
Most properties at The Place at Corkscrew are in Flood Zone X, meaning flood insurance is not required for federally backed mortgages. The community is inland off Corkscrew Road, and many listings specifically highlight "no flood zone" as a feature. Always verify on the FEMA Flood Map for any specific property.
What is the HOA fee at The Place at Corkscrew? +
The Place at Corkscrew is known for relatively low HOA fees that cover community maintenance and amenities access. There is also a CDD (Community Development District) assessment on the property tax bill. Buyers should always confirm the current total of both HOA fees and CDD assessments on the specific property tax record when evaluating any home.
Is The Place at Corkscrew a 55+ community? +
No. The Place at Corkscrew is an all-ages community. Its amenities — including the waterslide, splash pad, playground, childwatch services, and dog park — are specifically designed for families and buyers of all ages.
