About the Area

The Heart of Southwest Florida Living

Estero sits at the geographic heart of Southwest Florida, making it one of the region's most strategically located and fastest-growing communities. Bordered by Bonita Springs to the south and Fort Myers to the north, Estero offers an exceptional collection of master-planned communities, championship golf courses, and natural preserves.

The community is anchored by some of Southwest Florida's most desirable neighborhoods — including Miromar Lakes, WildBlue, and Raptor Bay — each offering gated security, resort-style amenities, and a quality of life that rivals communities at significantly higher price points.

With Florida Gulf Coast University, Miromar Outlets, and Hertz Arena all within the village, Estero blends leisure, culture, and convenience in a way few communities can match.

Why Buyers Choose Estero

Estero residents enjoy a lifestyle centered around resort-style community amenities, championship golf, and natural beauty — all within minutes of exceptional dining, shopping, and entertainment.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the most popular communities in Estero? +
Miromar Lakes is consistently ranked among the top communities in the country and centers on 700 acres of freshwater lakes with a private beach club. Raptor Bay, Grandezza, WildBlue, and The Reserve at Estero are also highly sought-after. For new construction, Verdana Village and Kingston are drawing significant buyer attention.
How are schools in Estero? +
Lee County Schools serves Estero with strong public options including Three Oaks Elementary and Estero High School, one of the district's most highly regarded. Charter options like Oak Creek Charter School offer additional choices, and Florida Gulf Coast University is located directly within the village.
Does Estero have flood risk? +
Most of Estero's master-planned communities are built on elevated land with engineered drainage, reducing flood risk compared to coastal areas. That said, flood zone status varies by property. Always verify using the FEMA Flood Map, and we walk every buyer through this as part of our standard process.
What is the new construction market like in Estero? +
Estero is one of Southwest Florida's most active new construction markets. Verdana Village, WildBlue, The Place at Corkscrew, and Kingston are among the most talked-about active communities. We strongly recommend speaking with us before visiting any sales center — builder reps work for the builder, not for you.
Is Estero an incorporated city? +
Estero became an incorporated village in 2014, giving it local governance and meaningful control over development decisions. The Village of Estero has been intentional about managing growth and protecting community character — a key reason its communities retain their appeal and value.
How close is Estero to the beach? +
Most Estero neighborhoods are 20–30 minutes from Bonita Beach, Lovers Key State Park, or Fort Myers Beach — all among Southwest Florida's finest Gulf-access beaches. Residents of Miromar Lakes also enjoy their own private freshwater beach club without ever leaving the community.
