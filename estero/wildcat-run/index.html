About the Community

Tucked Away in the Middle of Everything —
Estero's Most Intimate Golf Club

Wildcat Run Golf & Country Club has been one of Estero's most distinctive private communities since it was established in 1985. With just 450 homes across the entire community, it has a scale and intimacy that you simply cannot find in the larger, newer master-planned developments that dominate the Corkscrew corridor — and that intimate size is exactly what makes it so appealing to a specific, loyal buyer profile.

The community's most significant 2026 advantage is its recently renovated Arnold Palmer Signature golf course — completed in 2023 — which now competes with any course in Southwest Florida at a membership cost that undercuts comparable Naples golf clubs by a wide margin. Combine this with the fact that there is currently no waitlist for membership, and Wildcat Run represents one of the best golf value propositions in the region.

The lots are another defining feature. Single-family homes at Wildcat Run typically sit on 0.25–0.5+ acre homesites — unusually large for an Estero community — surrounded by mature palms, oaks, and cypress preserves that took decades to establish. You won't find that level of established beauty in a newer community for any amount of money.

The Case for Wildcat Run

Why Buyers Choose Wildcat Run
Over Newer Golf Communities

Intimate Scale

At 450 homes, Wildcat Run is a fraction of the size of Verdana Village or Bella Terra. Members know each other. The social experience is genuinely community-driven — not anonymous. For buyers coming from Northeast country clubs, this familiarity is exactly what they're looking for.

Oversized Lots

Single-family lots averaging 0.25–0.5+ acres are almost impossible to find in newer Estero developments. The separation between homes, the space for landscaping, and the sense of privacy that comes from a large lot takes decades to develop — and no amount of money can accelerate it in a new community.

Golf Value

The membership cost at Wildcat Run is a fraction of comparable Naples clubs like Grey Oaks or Mediterra. With the course now fully renovated and no waitlist, buyers can enter one of Southwest Florida's best private golf experiences immediately — at a total club + real estate cost that simply doesn't exist in Naples.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a waitlist for golf membership at Wildcat Run? +
No — as of 2026, there is no waitlist for golf membership at Wildcat Run. Qualified buyers can purchase membership immediately. This is a meaningful advantage compared to many comparable communities in Estero and Naples where 1–3 year membership waitlists are common. Contact us for current initiation fee structures and membership types.
When was the Wildcat Run golf course renovated? +
The Arnold Palmer Signature golf course was comprehensively renovated in 2023. The renovation addressed course conditioning, drainage, bunkering, and the practice facility. The renovated course now competes with any private course in Southwest Florida and is considered one of the region's finest layouts by members who play broadly across the area.
How large are the lots at Wildcat Run? +
Single-family homes at Wildcat Run typically sit on 0.25 to 0.5+ acre homesites — significantly larger than what you'll find in newer master-planned communities on the Corkscrew corridor. Some estate lots approach a full acre. The combination of large lots and mature landscaping established since 1985 creates a level of privacy and beauty that simply cannot be replicated in newer communities.
What dining options are available at Wildcat Run? +
Wildcat Run has two on-site dining venues. The formal dining room in the main clubhouse hosts special events and fine dining. The renovated Grill Room is a casual, social space with expanded indoor and outdoor seating, fire pits overlooking the bocce courts, and a relaxed atmosphere that serves as the daily social hub. Both are member-only.
How does Wildcat Run compare to Grandezza or Raptor Bay? +
All three are established Estero golf communities. Grandezza offers bundled golf with membership included in the purchase price. Raptor Bay has access to the Hyatt Regency Coconut Point resort. Wildcat Run's distinction is its scale (just 450 homes vs. 400+ at Grandezza), lot size, and the Arnold Palmer course prestige. Membership cost at Wildcat Run is generally competitive with Grandezza but the community has a more intimate, private club feel. We're happy to do a detailed side-by-side for any buyer comparing these communities.
Is Wildcat Run a good investment? +
Wildcat Run estate homes have demonstrated strong value retention, particularly well-renovated homes on fairway and lakefront lots. The combination of the 2023 course renovation, no membership waitlist, and value-per-square-foot advantage relative to Naples golf clubs has made Wildcat Run increasingly attractive to buyers who are buying and comparing. Rental demand for golf communities is strong on a seasonal basis, though HOA rental policies should be confirmed before any investment purchase.
