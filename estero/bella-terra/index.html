About the Community

Big Amenities. Reasonable Prices.
Active Community Life.

Bella Terra is one of Estero's most established and beloved gated communities — a 1,000-acre Lennar development on Corkscrew Road that has been fully built out and is now a vibrant, mature neighborhood with beautiful landscaping, established trees, and a genuine community spirit you can feel the moment you drive through the gate.

The community's roughly 1,900–2,000 homes span every type: condominiums in the Barletta enclave, attached villas, coach homes, and single-family homes ranging from 1,600 to 3,800 SF. This range makes Bella Terra one of the most accessible entry points into gated Estero living, while still delivering an amenity campus that rivals communities at twice the price.

Drive through Bella Terra at any hour and you'll see what makes it work: bikers, walkers, kids heading to the pool, families on the ball fields. The activity level is high, the community events calendar is full, and the HOA keeps the grounds in excellent condition. For buyers who want gated security, strong amenities, and reasonable pricing — Bella Terra delivers all three.

What "Fully Built Out" Means for Buyers

Lennar completed Bella Terra — no new construction sales are available. This is actually a meaningful advantage for many buyers: the landscaping is mature, the neighborhood character is fully established, there's no construction noise or disruption, and you can see exactly what you're buying.

In the resale market, Bella Terra's inventory typically includes a mix of condos, villas, and single-family homes at prices that represent outstanding value compared to comparable gated communities. Move-in-ready, upgraded homes frequently come to market — often fully furnished as seasonal properties returning to sale.

Resale Advantage

More room to negotiate than new construction.

Unlike new construction where the builder sets the price, Bella Terra resales give buyers real negotiating power — especially on homes that have sat on the market. What the Fees Actually Cost You

No CDD. HOA covers the resort amenities.

Bella Terra has no CDD fees — a meaningful advantage over many Estero communities. The HOA covers maintenance of the resort amenity package. We walk every buyer through the true cost of ownership — HOA by unit type, property taxes, insurance — before you fall in love with a floor plan.

Community Intelligence

Not all streets and sections are equal.

Bella Terra has condos, villas, and single-family homes across multiple sections with different HOA structures and lot positions. We know which sections have preserve views vs. road noise, which have the best walkability to amenities, and which have had the strongest resale appreciation.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bella Terra still building new construction? +
No. Bella Terra by Lennar is fully built out — all purchases are resale. This creates a mature community with beautiful landscaping, established trees, and no construction activity. For buyers who want brand-new construction on the Corkscrew corridor, Verdana Village, WildBlue, and The Place at Corkscrew are active communities we also represent.
What types of homes are available at Bella Terra? +
Bella Terra offers three distinct housing types. The Barletta enclave features condominiums from the $200s (1,055–1,320 SF). Attached villas range from the $300s (1,620+ SF with 2-car garage). Single-family homes run from the mid-$300s to $700K+ (1,600–3,800 SF). All home types have full access to the same amenity campus.
Is Bella Terra family-friendly? +
It's one of Estero's most family-oriented communities. The amenity campus includes multi-use ball fields (soccer, softball), basketball courts, an inline skating rink, splash pad, butterfly garden, and a playground alongside resort pools and tennis. The community hosts regular events, and the neighborhood's activity level — walkers, bikers, kids — reflects a genuinely engaged resident base.
How does Bella Terra compare to newer communities like Verdana Village? +
Bella Terra is fully built out with established landscaping and mature trees — the community feels settled and complete in a way that newer communities can't replicate yet. Price points are generally lower than comparable newer gated options, making it the best value per square foot in gated Estero. The primary trade-off is that new construction options are not available; buyers are choosing from resale inventory. We can walk you through a direct comparison of all Corkscrew corridor communities.
What is the HOA fee at Bella Terra? +
HOA fees at Bella Terra vary by home type — condos, villas, and single-family homes carry different assessments. Fees cover amenity maintenance, common areas, gated security, and landscaping. Contact us for current fee information on any specific home type you're considering — we review HOA documents as a standard part of our buyer process.
Is Bella Terra a good investment property? +
Bella Terra has proven rental demand — its combination of amenities, location, and price point makes it attractive for seasonal rentals and long-term tenants alike. Investors should verify HOA rental policy before purchasing, as some communities have minimum rental periods. Contact us for current rental rules and a realistic income assessment for any property you're evaluating.
