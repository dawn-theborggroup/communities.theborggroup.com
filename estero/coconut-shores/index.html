About the Community

Living in Coconut Shores

What is it like to live in Coconut Shores? It is quiet, green and close to everything. This small, gated community sits off Coconut Road just west of US 41, and many homes look out over lakes with fountains and wooded islands. Built from 2000 to 2002, it feels settled, with mature palms and pines throughout.

Life here centers on the amenity center by the lake: a resort-style pool and spa, a clubhouse with a fitness room and lending library, a tiki hut for community events, a fishing pier with a thatched gazebo, and tennis and pickleball courts. Step outside the gate and Coconut Point’s shops, restaurants and movie theater are right across US 41.

Is it a good fit for a seasonal home or a full-time one? Both. The low-maintenance villas and condos suit snowbirds who want to lock and leave, and full-time owners who want amenities and a central Estero location without a large yard to care for.

Good to know

Pet and rental rules differ between the villa and condo associations and can change. We confirm the current rules, fees and any special assessments for every buyer and seller we work with.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Coconut Shores in Estero? +
Coconut Shores is a small, gated community of attached villas and condos off Coconut Road in Estero, Florida, built from 2000 to 2002 around lakes, fountains and a resort-style amenity center. It sits just west of US 41, across from Coconut Point.
Is Coconut Shores gated? +
Yes. Coconut Shores is a gated community.
What kinds of homes are in Coconut Shores? +
Two kinds. Attached villas on Coconut Shores Drive are mostly three-bedroom, two-bath plans of about 1,700 square feet with a two-car garage and a screened lanai. Condos on Coconut Island Drive, South Coconut Island Drive and Morning Lake Drive are two-bedroom plans of roughly 1,740 to 2,050 square feet in two-story buildings.
What amenities does Coconut Shores have? +
A resort-style pool and spa, a clubhouse with a fitness room, lending library and community kitchen, a tiki hut that hosts community events, a lakeside fishing pier with a thatched gazebo, a barbecue area, and tennis and pickleball courts.
What are the association fees in Coconut Shores? +
Villa association costs run about $5,412 a year, or about $1,353 a quarter. Condo building associations run roughly $8,904 to $9,260 a year, depending on the building. Fees change, so confirm the current figures and any special assessments before you make an offer. Lenders count association dues in a buyer's monthly payment, so the villa and condo difference can change what a buyer qualifies for.
What is near Coconut Shores? +
Coconut Point's shops, restaurants and movie theater are across US 41. Hertz Arena, Southwest Florida International Airport and the beaches of Bonita Springs and Fort Myers Beach are a short drive away.
Can I see what homes in Coconut Shores are selling for? +
Yes. Our Coconut Shores market report shows recent closings, homes for sale and homes under contract, with the attached villas and the condos reported separately.
