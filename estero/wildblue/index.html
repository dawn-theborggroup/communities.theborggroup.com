About the Community

Where Real Lake Living Meets
Resort-Style Amenities

WildBlue occupies a unique position in the Southwest Florida new construction market: it is built around water in the way most communities only claim to be. The three freshwater lakes at the heart of the community are not the decorative retention ponds that carry the word "lake" in most subdivision names — they are genuine open-water lakes covering over 800 acres where residents powerboat, kayak, paddleboard, sail, and fish from their own backyard docks.

Set on approximately 3,500 acres in Estero, WildBlue pairs that lake lifestyle with 1,329 acres of protected nature preserve — meaning views from a significant portion of homesites will never be developed. The amenity campus sits on its own 20-acre peninsula jutting into WildBlue Lake, with a resort pool, indoor and outdoor dining, fitness and spa, and courts for every sport.

Four builders serve different buyer profiles: Lennar and Pulte at accessible price points, WCI at the luxury tier, and Stock Development at the custom estate level up to $4 million and beyond — giving WildBlue an unusual ability to serve buyers from first-time luxury purchasers to established high-net-worth buyers within the same community.

What Sets WildBlue Apart

These Are Real Lakes

Most Southwest Florida communities use the word "lake" to describe retention ponds. WildBlue's lakes are the genuine article — and that difference is the entire story.

Three Open-Water Lakes
800+ Acres Total

WildBlue's three freshwater lakes span over 800 acres of open water — large enough for powerboats, sailboats, and all water sports. The main WildBlue Lake sits directly in front of the amenity peninsula and provides the kind of open-water views that are genuinely rare in any inland Florida community.

Private Docks & Marina
Lakefront Homeowners

Lakefront home purchases at WildBlue can include private docks behind the home. The community marina offers a boat launch and dock access for all residents. Whether you want to fish from your yard at sunrise or spend the afternoon paddleboarding, the infrastructure is already in place.

Water Sports & Activities
Year-Round

Powerboating, kayaking, paddleboarding, canoeing, sailing, and fishing are all available on WildBlue's lakes year-round. The community provides kayaks and paddleboards for resident use, and the warm freshwater lakes make water sports accessible every month of the year.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is WildBlue in Estero Florida? +
WildBlue is a 3,500-acre master-planned gated community in Estero, Florida built around over 800 acres of real navigable freshwater lakes. Approximately 1,000 single-family homes are built by Lennar, Pulte, WCI, and Stock Development, with prices from the $700s to $4 million and above. The amenity campus sits on a 20-acre peninsula overlooking WildBlue Lake.
Can you have a boat at WildBlue? +
Yes. WildBlue's freshwater lakes are large enough for powerboating, kayaking, paddleboarding, sailing, canoeing, and fishing. The community has a marina, boat launch, and docks. Lakefront homeowners can have private docks. This is one of the very few inland Southwest Florida communities where true on-water motorized boating is available.
What builders are in WildBlue? +
WildBlue features four builders: Lennar ($700s–$1.2M), Pulte ($700s–$1.3M+), WCI — Lennar's luxury brand ($800s–$2M+), and Stock Development for custom estate homes ($2M–$4M+). Always bring your own agent before visiting any of the builder sales offices — representation costs you nothing and protects your interests throughout the contract and closing process.
Is WildBlue a 55+ community? +
No. WildBlue is an all-ages community with no age restrictions. It attracts families, active adults, seasonal residents, and full-time retirees across all life stages.
What is the difference between WildBlue and Vista WildBlue? +
Vista WildBlue is a distinct neighborhood section within the broader WildBlue community featuring Lennar's luxury home offerings at higher price points. Both share the same amenity campus and lake access. Vista WildBlue homes tend to be larger and more premium-finished, positioned at the upper tier of the Lennar product line. Both are served by the same HOA and amenity infrastructure.
How far is WildBlue from the beach? +
WildBlue is located inland off Corkscrew Road, approximately 20 miles from Fort Myers Beach and 25 miles from Bonita Beach. RSW International Airport is approximately 15 minutes away. Coconut Point Mall, Miromar Outlets, and Gulf Coast Town Center are all within a short drive.
