About the Community

Living in Corkscrew Estates

Corkscrew Estates is one of the smallest and most private communities in the Corkscrew Road corridor. Rather than a large amenity campus, it offers estate living: gated entry, nearly one-acre homesites, and large Pulte-built homes with three to six bedrooms and up to three-car garages.

Homes were built in recent years, so buyers are typically looking at newer construction on generous lots rather than older resales. With a small number of homes and a thin resale market, each sale tends to stand on its own condition, lot and upgrades rather than on a community amenity package.

Good to know

Corkscrew Estates is a quiet, low-density alternative to the amenity-heavy communities nearby. If a resort-style pool, restaurant and fitness center matter most, compare it with The Place at Corkscrew or Corkscrew Shores.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Corkscrew Estates in Estero? +
Corkscrew Estates is a small gated community of estate homes built by Pulte Homes off Corkscrew Road in Estero, Florida, with homes on nearly one-acre lots.
Is Corkscrew Estates gated? +
Yes. Corkscrew Estates is a gated community.
How big are the homes and lots at Corkscrew Estates? +
Homes range from roughly 2,600 to more than 4,300 square feet with three to six bedrooms and up to three-car garages, on nearly one-acre lots.
Does Corkscrew Estates have a clubhouse or pool? +
Corkscrew Estates is an estate-style enclave rather than an amenity community and is not known for a clubhouse or resort amenity package. Buyers who want one often compare it with nearby communities such as The Place at Corkscrew.
What are the HOA fees at Corkscrew Estates? +
HOA dues vary by property and can change. Confirm the current HOA and any CDD or special assessments on the tax record and with the association before making an offer.
