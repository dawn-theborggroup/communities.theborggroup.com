The Quick Answer

What is The Brooks?

The Brooks is a 2,492-acre master-planned community in Estero, developed by the Bonita Bay Group between U.S. 41 and I-75 around Coconut Road. It holds five distinct gated villages — Shadow Wood, Spring Run, Copperleaf, Lighthouse Bay, and Pebble Pointe — with more than 3,600 residences set among preserves, lakes, and four golf courses. In its heyday it ranked among the best-selling master-planned communities in the entire country, and its streets today show why: mature, settled, and beautifully kept.

The key to understanding The Brooks is that it isn't one community — it's five, each with a different answer to the questions that matter most: Do you want golf bundled, optional, or not at all? Estate home or lock-and-leave coach home? Country club or waterfront resort? Choosing your village is choosing your lifestyle, and the rest of this guide walks you through each one.

The Five Villages

Which village of The Brooks fits your life?

The Flagship · Optional Golf

Shadow Wood

The country-club heart of The Brooks — nearly 1,500 residences across 34 neighborhoods, from coach homes to custom estates, with 36 holes inside the gates and golf as an optional equity membership. Nine lighted clay tennis courts and a classic club calendar round it out.

Bundled Golf

Spring Run

About 850 homes across 20 neighborhoods where an 18-hole golf membership is bundled with ownership — the turn-key golf lifestyle, with condos and single-family homes built for low-maintenance living.

Bundled Golf · Lowest Density

Copperleaf

The most intimate of the golf villages at 565 residences, with its own bundled 18-hole course, a tight-knit social scene, and a mix of condominiums and single-family homes.

All Waterfront · No Golf

Lighthouse Bay

The resort village — 650 homes and every single one on the water, wrapped around 13 lakes with a lagoon-style pool, lap pool, tennis, and a boathouse. Condos, coach homes, carriage homes, and villas.

The Newest · No Golf

Pebble Pointe

A newer enclave of 90 single-family homes with modern designs and no golf obligations — the quiet, contemporary corner of The Brooks.

The Commons Club

What ties the five villages together?

The Commons Club — the community-wide private club every Brooks resident is eligible to join, headquartered on Coconut Road between Copperleaf and Shadow Wood. Members enjoy The Rookery, a private restaurant; a fitness center with spa services; an enrichment center running classes, programs, and a library; pickleball and bocce courts; a fishing pier; and miles of walking and biking trails through the preserves.

And then there's the beach. The Commons Club's private Beach Club sits on Little Hickory Island between Bonita Beach and Lovers Key — cabanas, chairs and umbrellas, a snack bar, kayaks and paddleboards, all waiting so members arrive empty-handed. Beach access comes with the club's Gold membership tier, which is capped at 1,575 members; that cap is exactly the kind of detail that changes a buying decision, and we check current availability for every Brooks buyer we work with.

Location

Where is The Brooks, and what's nearby?

First, the question that confuses everyone online: The Brooks is in Estero, though some addresses within it carry Bonita Springs ZIP codes. What matters is where it actually sits — squarely in the middle of the corridor, between U.S. 41 and I-75 around Coconut Road. Coconut Point's shopping and dining are essentially next door, RSW airport is an easy drive up I-75, Bonita Beach is a short trip west, and both downtown Naples and downtown Fort Myers are within half an hour. Few communities in Southwest Florida are this connected while feeling this tucked away.

Homes & The Fine Print

What do homes cost, and how do the fees work?

The range across the villages is wide: condominiums, coach homes, and villas generally start in the high $200,000s, single-family homes commonly run from the $600,000s into the low millions, and Shadow Wood's custom estates climb well beyond. But in The Brooks, the sticker price is only half the math — a bundled-golf home in Spring Run, an optional-equity-golf home in Shadow Wood, and a no-golf waterfront home in Lighthouse Bay carry completely different monthly and annual obligations, before you even add Commons Club tiers from Bronze to Gold.

That's not a complication; it's an opportunity — it means The Brooks has a village and a fee structure for almost every lifestyle and budget in the luxury market. Our job is putting the exact total-cost numbers side by side for the villages you're drawn to, so the home you fall for is also the math that fits.

Common Questions

The Brooks, Answered

What are the five villages of The Brooks?
The Brooks is made up of five gated villages, each with its own personality: Shadow Wood (the country-club flagship with custom estates and optional golf), Spring Run (bundled golf with low-maintenance living), Copperleaf (bundled golf at the community's lowest density), Lighthouse Bay (the non-golf, all-waterfront resort village where every home has a lake view), and Pebble Pointe (the newest enclave of single-family homes with no golf obligations).
Does The Brooks have private beach access?
Yes — through the Commons Club. Its private Beach Club sits on Little Hickory Island between Bonita Beach and Lovers Key, with cabanas, chairs and umbrellas, a snack bar, kayaks, paddleboards, and beach bocce waiting for members. Beach Club access requires the Commons Club's Gold membership, which is capped at 1,575 members — so availability is worth checking early in your search.
Is golf membership required in The Brooks?
It depends entirely on which village you choose — and that's the beauty of The Brooks. Spring Run and Copperleaf bundle golf into ownership. Shadow Wood makes its 36 holes an optional equity membership. Lighthouse Bay and Pebble Pointe have no golf attached at all. Same master community, four completely different answers — which is why village choice is the first conversation we have with Brooks buyers.
What is the Commons Club at The Brooks?
The Commons Club is the community-wide private club every Brooks resident is eligible to join, headquartered on Coconut Road between Copperleaf and Shadow Wood. It offers The Rookery private restaurant, a fitness center with spa services, an enrichment center with classes and programs, pickleball, bocce, a fishing pier, and walking trails — with membership tiers from Bronze to Gold, and Gold unlocking the private beach club.
What do homes cost in The Brooks?
The range is wide because the villages are so different: condominiums, coach homes, and villas generally start in the high $200,000s, single-family homes commonly run in the $600,000s to low millions, and Shadow Wood's custom estates reach well beyond that. Bundled-golf villages carry different fee math than optional-golf or non-golf villages, so comparing homes across villages means comparing total cost of ownership — something we put exact numbers on for every buyer.
Is The Brooks in Estero or Bonita Springs?
The Brooks is located in Estero, between U.S. 41 and I-75 around Coconut Road — though some addresses within it carry Bonita Springs ZIP codes, which causes plenty of confusion online. Either way, it sits at the center of the corridor: Coconut Point is minutes away, RSW airport is an easy drive north, and Bonita Beach is a short trip west.
