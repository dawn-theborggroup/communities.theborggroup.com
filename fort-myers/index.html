About the Area

River District Culture Meets
Gulf Coast Living

Fort Myers is the cultural and commercial heart of Southwest Florida — a city that offers something genuinely rare on the Gulf Coast: the energy of a real downtown alongside world-class waterfront living. The River District anchors the city with restaurants, galleries, live music, and a revitalized waterfront that draws both residents and visitors year-round.

Beyond downtown, Fort Myers stretches across a wide range of neighborhoods — from gated golf and marina communities to family-friendly master-planned developments near the airport corridor. The Caloosahatchee River and its tributaries create a natural boating network that connects residents to the Gulf, the islands, and everything between.

Fort Myers Beach — on Estero Island to the southwest — is in the midst of a meaningful rebuilding effort following Hurricane Ian in 2022. New construction is rising on the island with stricter, storm-resilient building codes, and many buyers see this as a rare chance to enter a Gulf-front market before supply normalizes.

Why Buyers Choose Fort Myers

Fort Myers gives buyers access to urban amenities, waterfront boating, barrier island beaches, and some of the region's most established gated communities — all within reach of Southwest Florida International Airport.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Fort Myers Beach recovering from Hurricane Ian? +
Recovery is ongoing and meaningful. New construction is actively underway across much of Estero Island with stricter, FEMA-compliant elevated construction standards. The Town of Fort Myers Beach is actively managing redevelopment permits and new businesses are reopening regularly. Many buyers and investors view this as one of the few remaining opportunities to purchase Gulf-front property in Southwest Florida before supply tightens.
What flood zone considerations should I know about in Fort Myers? +
Fort Myers has a wide range of flood zones. Coastal areas, riverfront properties, and Fort Myers Beach carry higher FEMA risk designations (AE and VE zones) — meaning higher flood insurance costs and more stringent elevation requirements. Inland neighborhoods and higher-elevation communities like Gateway have significantly lower exposure. Always verify flood zone and base flood elevation data on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center before making an offer.
What is the difference between bundled and equity golf communities? +
Bundled golf means golf membership is included with your HOA fees — no additional buy-in required. Colonial Country Club uses this model. Equity golf means you purchase a membership separately, often for $50,000–$150,000 or more, which gives you voting rights and equity in the club. Both have advantages depending on how much you play and how involved you want to be in club governance.
How far is Fort Myers from Naples and Bonita Springs? +
Central Fort Myers is approximately 20–25 minutes north of Bonita Springs and 35–40 minutes north of downtown Naples via I-75 or US-41. RSW International Airport sits between Fort Myers and Estero, making it highly accessible from all points in the corridor. Fort Myers Beach is about 25 minutes from downtown Fort Myers.
Is Fort Myers a good place for families? +
Yes — Fort Myers has strong family-oriented neighborhoods throughout. Gateway, Reflection Lakes, and many south Fort Myers communities are particularly popular with families, offering larger lots, good schools, and access to sports leagues and parks. Lee Health's HealthPark campus with the Golisano Children's Hospital provides specialized pediatric care in-county.
How far is Fort Myers from Sanibel and Captiva? +
Sanibel and Captiva Islands are approximately 20–25 minutes from central Fort Myers via the Sanibel Causeway — among the most beautiful and ecologically protected barrier islands in Florida. Sanibel is famous for world-class shelling, and both islands draw nature lovers, kayakers, and beach enthusiasts from across Southwest Florida.
